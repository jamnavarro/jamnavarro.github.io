Hey, I'm Jam! Raised in Kansas City, born in the Philippines. I have a passion for exploring different ways to tell stories of our lived experiences and am always curious to understand why we do the things we do.

Right now, I'm a UI/UX designer for digital interactive installations in experience centers, universities, and museums at Dimensional Innovations. I'm also a research program coordinator for psychedelic science at the Johns Hopkins Center for Psychedelic and Consciousness Research.

Day-to-day outside of work, you can find me making or listening to music, playing all sorts of video games, journaling, or reading up on design inspiration.
